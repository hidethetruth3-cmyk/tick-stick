# Tick Stick (Fabric 1.21.11)
/tickstick give 8            8 "Here" sticks (target goes where you stand)
/tickstick give aim 30       30 "Aim" sticks (target goes where your crosshair points)
/tickstick give here 5
/tickstick tp <player>       teleport that player with the stick you hold (counts as used on that stick)
Right-click: random OTHER player. Unlimited uses, but each person only once per stick. New stick = they can be teleported again.
